How to read these comparisons

History, quotes and predictions

Cards show past statement totals. The middle 50% is the 25th–75th percentile of those observations, rounded outwards to whole dollars; it is not a forecast interval. The typical value is the historical median, rounded to a whole dollar.

A typed quote is an observation supplied by you, not a live connection. No model has been validated and no prediction accuracy is claimed.

Comparable, with limits

Provider and Grab service never substitute for one another. We prefer an available hour sample, then weekday, then all observed times within the same service. Hour and weekday are not jointly matched. Cross-provider comparisons use the same available grouping.

Five observations permit a descriptive comparison, not statistical confidence. Different dates, traffic, route variations, offers and fees may explain differences. Small samples do not establish a cheaper provider or a better booking time.

Multi-stop & private places

V Place remains an alias. Reviewed ordered stops can form a separate route; ambiguous imported stops are held for local review. Older aggregates do not establish whether a journey was direct. Endpoint-only distance is never used for a verified multi-stop route.

Small groups are withheld and only approved place aliases are published. Public aggregates still reveal habits. The Old Dashboard uses the same restricted dataset.

Proposed next step: a private manual quote log

Proposal only — no quote logger is enabled. A useful first version would record an alias-only ordered route, service, observation time, quoted range, fee inclusion, chosen option and a separately entered final paid fare. This captures rejected quotes as well as the booked option.

Preferred pilot: explicit opt-in, on-device storage with delete/export controls, no GPS in log records, no screenshots or receipt uploads, no background sync. Device access and backups can still expose it; browser storage is not encrypted by this app. A paired comparison needs quotes captured close together, and metered estimates must retain their range. Logger storage needs your approval before implementation; the optional place setup above saves only location anchors.
